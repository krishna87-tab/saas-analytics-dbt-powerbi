# Executive Dashboard Design

## Objective

The Executive Dashboard provides senior leadership with a consolidated view of SaaS business performance.

## KPIs

- Revenue
- Active Customers
- Revenue at Risk
- Payment Success Rate
- At-Risk Customers
- Average Health Score

## Dashboard Sections

### Executive KPIs
Provides a snapshot of overall business performance.

### Revenue Trend
Displays monthly revenue growth.

### Customer Health
Shows customer distribution across health categories.

### Regional Performance
Compares customer health by region.

### Risk Drivers
Highlights the primary factors contributing to customer risk.

### Customer Details
Provides drill-down capability for executive analysis.
